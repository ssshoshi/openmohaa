# Content paks

Game data changes that go with the engine, one folder per pak. Each folder
builds into `zzzzzzzzz-<folder>.pk3`, which sorts after the retail and mod paks
and so overrides them, or, when the folder has a `LOAD_LAST` file, into
`zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz-<folder>.pk3`, after the MOHAIM addon too.

Most of these change a retail or mod file that is not ours to publish, so the
folder holds only a unified diff against it (`<path>.patch`). The build takes
the original from your own paks, the copy the game would load (the last pak in
load order that has it), and applies the diff. Files from elsewhere that are
not ours either are listed with where to download them (`*.fetch`, one
`<path in the pak> <url>` a line), and the build fetches them. Files of our own
go in as they are, at their path under the folder. Images made from the
game's own are recipes (`*.derive`, one `<path in the pak> <game path> scale <k>`
a line: the game's image with its colours times k), made at build time, and so
are animations made from the game's own (`*.additive`, one
`<path in the pak> <pose .skc> <motion .skc>` a line: the pose's first frame
with its upper body moving as the motion's does).

    tools/content/pack.py                    # every pak, into build/content/
    tools/content/pack.py opm-hrrtm-blood    # one of them
    tools/content/pack.py --install "/path/to/main"

`MOHAA_GAME` (or `--game`) is the folder holding `main/*.pk3`, by default
`/mnt/d/Medal of Honor`. To make a diff from an edited copy of a game file:

    tools/content/pack.py --diff <folder> <path in the pak> <edited file>

| Pak | Needs | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `opm-atmos` | nothing | A map's own air (`atmos/<map>.txt`, read by `tr_atmos.c`): so far its wind, where it blows to, how hard and how gusty, for the campaign's outdoor maps and the multiplayer ones. The grass leans with it and the rain, the snow and the smoke are carried on it. All our own; without the pak every map has the one wind there always was. |
| `opm-vis` | nothing | What a map's compiled visibility leaves out, put back (`vis/<map>.txt`, read by `cm_load.c`'s `CMod_LoadVisPatch`): one `see <x y z> <x y z> ...` a line, written from `viewpos`, and the first point's area and each later point's then see each other. m1l1's town wall has invisible vis blockers on it up to well above its top, so the telegraph poles over the roofs and the dome beyond the wall came and went as the player stepped. All our own; without the pak those pop as they always did. |
| `opm-cabinet-ragdoll` | retail | The man who falls out of a shot hidden cabinet (`global/cabinet.scr`, m3l2, m5l1a, m5l1b) dies once he is clear of it, so the ragdoll takes over his fall. |
| `opm-hrrtm-blood` | HRRTM Blood Effects Addon | Bullet hits on people splatter the wall behind at once and at full strength, light hits as well as hard ones. Built from the addon's own effect, so without the addon it is not built. Its two wall splats three times brighter (`blood.derive`): the addon's are near black. |
| `opm-crouch-idle` | retail | The player crouched no longer holds still: the crouch idles the player model plays (`models/player/base/anims_*.txt`) were held poses, and are now each crouch with the upper body breathing and shifting as the standing idle of the same weapon does (`crouch_idles.additive`, made from the game's anims at build time). The body, its shadow and the view weapon's idle sway (`vm_idle`, which follows the body's hand) move crouched as they do standing. The legs stay as they were. Loads after the MOHAIM addon (`LOAD_LAST`: `zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz-` prefix), whose own `anims_*.txt` would otherwise win. |
| `opm-gore` | nothing | The gore system's shaders and textures (`cg_gore.cpp`): wounds, drops, splashes and pools. All our own; the textures are drawn by `tools/content/gore_textures.py`. |
| `opm-physics` | internet, once | What corpses and props sound like striking things: the `phys_*` aliases the physics plays (`code/physics/phys_impact.cpp`), on Half-Life 2's and Day of Defeat: Source's physics recordings. Those are Valve's, so `physics.fetch` lists where the build downloads them from (kept in `~/.cache/openmohaa-content`) and only the pak built here has them. |
| `opm-water` | nothing | How a map's water looks and moves (`water/<map>.txt`, read by `tr_water.c` and `cg_water.cpp`), and m3l1a's: grey-green Channel water, a swell rolling in onto Omaha, blood in the surf, the falling tide's wet sand with water standing on it and red under the dead, and the men of the first waves lying in the surf (`maps/m3l1a_opm.scr`), whom the waves lift, roll and wash up the beach. Patched: the bullet splash in water (`bh_water_lite`/`_hard.tik`: a column and a crown of drops, the rings left to the water itself) and m3l1a's scripted MG hits (`bhit`), which now show a real round's wound and blood (`global/opm_realhit.scr`, our own). |
| `opm-lamp-states` | retail; the lamp models pak for the glass | Lamps on models that go on and off. The swinging lantern put out (`models/lights/swinging_lantern.tik`'s `light_off`, m1l1's truck when the soldier turns it off) goes dark: the remodelled glass, drawn lit from inside, takes its second skin (`opm_swinging_lantern_glass_off`, lit by what is round it) and the remodel's flame is hidden; before, only the attached flame went and the glass glowed on. The swinging lantern lit has a bigger swaying candle flame set on its wick (`models/fx/opm_lanternflame.tik`, from opm-lamps, in place of the game's `lanternflame.tik`) and not the remodel's pulsing teardrop, and its cut-out card (`glanterncull`, which carries the handle) takes a texture with the glass cut away (`lantern.generate`: `tools/lamps/lantern_cull.py`, from the player's paks): the 4x upscale's noise let a third of that glass through, a speckled grey plane the flame clipped into. The Opel truck's body (`models/vehicles/opeltruck.tik`'s `opel1`) gets a second skin with its blackout headlight slits glowing (`opm_glow_opel`), which the renderer picks while its live headlights are on (lamps.txt `glowsurface`). |
| `opm-weapons` | your own weapon models (`models/<weapon>/` in the game folder); Blender and 7-Zip for the .fbx and .7z ones | The rest of the game's weapons remade from models of the player's own (`remodel.generate`: `tools/weapons/remodel.py`, one spec a weapon in `tools/weapons/weapon_specs.py`; the sources are read in place, never committed): the Colt, P38, Hi-Standard, Kar98k and its sniper, Springfield, MP40, StG 44, BAR, trench gun, bazooka, Panzerschreck and both grenades (the thrown ones too). Each is fitted onto the game's model (scaled ICP from every axis-aligned and principal-axis start) and every part of it takes the bone and surface of the game's part nearest it, so the game's animations and the .tik's frame commands (hide the magazine for the reload) move and hide it as they did; surfaces are named `<game surface>_<material>_<n>`, shaded by one wildcard line a group (the setup takes 24 lines, a model 32 surfaces). Folders' `Notes.txt` decide what is kept (no BAR bipod or handle; the Kar98k with and without its ZF scope; the Springfield's short scope). The hand's magazines are the new ones where the source has one. The Kar98k's and MP40's sights are put on the aim pose's line. GL2 gets normal and specular maps as the Thompson's. Loads after the MOHAIM addon (`LOAD_LAST`); a weapon whose folder is missing keeps the game's. |
| `opm-windows` | retail; torch for the sharper textures (else Lanczos) | Lit windows remade (`jh_rustwin1lit`, the lighthouse's; `window4_exteriorlit`, `jh_window1pj_lit`, `ihwin1a_framelit`; m1l1 has all four, and m1l2a/b, m1l3a/b, m2l1, m2l3, mohdm7 some): the frame 4x sharper (Real-ESRGAN), the glass lit from the room behind it (its blotches smoothed, brighter high in the middle, darker into each pane's corners, a fine frost) and blooming (`q3map_opmglow`, GL2's: only the added glass stage is brighter than white), and a night sky's reflection on the glass alone (diagonal glints that slide over it as you move, screened over the glass so it does not bloom). `windows.generate` runs `tools/windows/windows.py`, which makes the textures from the player's own paks; `scripts/light.shader.patch` points the four shaders at them. |
| `opm-garand` | your own M1 Garand model (`models/Garand/*.glb` in the game folder) | The M1 Garand remade from a model of the player's own (`garand.generate`: `tools/weapons/garand.py`, which reads the glTF in place: the rifle and an en-bloc clip of eight rounds with their PBR maps, never committed). The game's Garand already moves its bolt and operating rod on a bone of their own and its clip on another, so the new rifle is fitted onto it (scaled ICP, as the Thompson is) and cut onto the same bones: its bolt and operating rod slide back on each shot and stay back when it is empty, on the game's own timing along the new receiver's line. Its rear aperture and front post are turned onto the line MOHAIM's aim pose looks down. The clip sits in the receiver under the bolt and shows while there are rounds (the game hid it but while the reload pushes it in), goes when the last shot throws it out, and is also the clip the hand brings in (`models/ammo/garand_clip_reload.tik`) and, without its rounds, the empty one thrown out (`models/ammo/garand_clip_empty.tik`). GL2 gets its normal map and a specular map made from its roughness and metalness. Loads after the MOHAIM addon (`LOAD_LAST`), whose `m1_garand.tik` it repoints; without the model the pak is empty and the game keeps its Garand. |
| `opm-prone` | retail; internet, once | The player model's prone animations (`models/player/base/anims_shared.txt`), which the legs play while lying down and crawling, so the shadow and first-person body lie down too, and the stance sounds (`ubersound/opm_prone.scr`): lying down, getting up, crawling, sprinting and the reload rustle, on Day of Defeat: Source's player recordings. Those are Valve's, so `prone.fetch` lists where the build downloads them from. Without the pak prone still works, standing up in the shadow and silent. |
| `opm-rain` | nothing | m5l1a starts in the rain (`maps/m5l1a_opm.scr`, our own): an add-on that sets `global/weather.scr`'s second pattern before the weather script reads it, so it eases on from there instead of from dry. m5l1b too (`maps/m5l1b_opm.scr`): with the weather the player left m5l1a in, or m5l1a's start when it is loaded by itself; its own script set that only after the weather script had started dry. Not a patch to `maps/m5l1a.scr`: saves keep script threads as offsets into it. The water pouring off roofs' eaves in the rain and its splash (`scripts/opm_rain.shader`, read by `tr_water.c`'s `R_RainRunoff`): the stream's texture is our own, drawn by `tools/content/rain_textures.py`; the splash is the game's. |
| `opm-snow` | nothing | What the cold adds on maps with snow (`cg_snow.cpp`): a man's breath showing in the cold air (`models/fx/opm_breath.tik`, `cg_breath`) and the print a boot leaves in snow (`opm/snow_footprint`, `cg_footprints`; the texture is drawn by `tools/content/snow_textures.py`). All our own. Without the pak there is neither; the snow lying and glittering (`r_snow`) is the renderer's and needs no pak. |
| `opm-thompson` | your own Thompson model (`models/Thompson/` in the game folder) | The Thompson remade from a model of the player's own (`thompson.generate`: `tools/weapons/thompson.py`, which reads it in place: one unrigged `.obj` and its PBR maps, never committed). The game's Thompson is rigid on one bone and the arms' animations carry it, so the new mesh is fitted onto it (scaled ICP: sights, grip and muzzle where the aim poses, the barrel tag and the hands expect them) and bound to that bone; its magazine is cut out as the `Clip` surface the reload hides, and is the magazine the hand brings in too (`models/ammo/thompson_clip.tik`). Its own sights are then turned onto the line MOHAIM's aim pose looks down (the eye placed from the arms' aim animation, the peep hole found by drawing the rear sight from there), about a degree. Its cocking knob gets a bone of its own (`opm_bolt`) and the gun's animations are copied with it added, open bolt like the real M1A1: held back while there are rounds, forward and back on each shot, left forward by the shot that empties the magazine (`fire_empty`, `idle_empty`), and pulled back by the left hand when an empty gun is reloaded (`reload_empty`, which `Weapon::StartReloading` now plays for an empty weapon that has one). GL2 gets its normal map and a specular map made from its roughness and metalness. Loads after the MOHAIM addon (`LOAD_LAST`), whose `thompsonsmg.tik` it repoints; without the model the pak is empty and the game keeps its Thompson. |
| `opm-trees` | retail; internet, once | Trees stay whole at every distance: the flat sprites the far trees were swapped for, which popped in and out (and their shadows with them) as you walked, are never drawn, and the full model no longer fades out to make way for them (`scripts/trees.shader`). The leaf planes meant only for up close still fade. The trees whose `.tik` had no `lod_scale 100` (oak, birch, juniper, the winter pines) get it, which the static-model LOD now honours, so a far tree keeps its trunk. | The trees and bushes of the bocage are grown anew (`flora.generate`: `tools/flora/flora.py` makes them when the pak is built): oak, beech (the game's common tree), birch, and the hedge shrubs hazel, blackthorn, buckthorn and hornbeam, each a trunk and branches with bark and sprays of leaves, to the original's height and spread. Their leaves are scans and their bark photographs, from ambientCG and Poly Haven (CC0, downloaded once into `~/.cache/openmohaa-content/flora`). GL2 draws them from the graphics card (`r_staticModelVao`) and sways them in the wind (`r_floraWind`). |
